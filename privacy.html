Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 12, 2026  ·  Last Updated: August 12, 2026

Fernway ("we," "our," or "us") built Sproutly as a hydration-tracking app. This Privacy Policy explains what information Sproutly collects, how it's used, and — most importantly — how much of it never leaves your device.

By using Sproutly, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

The short version: Sproutly doesn't have user accounts, doesn't run its own servers, and doesn't use any analytics, advertising, or tracking software. Everything you log stays on your device (and, if you choose, in Apple Health). We never see it.

1. Information We Collect

Information stored on your device

Sproutly stores the following locally on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device SwiftData storage, shared only between the Sproutly app and its Home Screen widget (never transmitted to us or anyone else):

  • Your hydration log — drink entries, amounts, and timestamps
  • Custom drink types and reminder schedules you create
  • Profile details you enter to calculate your hydration goal, such as weight, gender, activity level, and climate
  • App preferences — theme, measurement units, notification settings

None of this is an "account" — there's no sign-up, no username, and no password. If you delete Sproutly from your device, this data is deleted with it.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you grant permission, Sproutly reads and writes to Apple Health:

  • Water intake: written to Apple Health when you log a drink, and read back to keep your daily total in sync — you can pause this at any time in Sproutly's own Settings without affecting any other Health permission.
  • Workouts: read (not written) to calculate an optional activity-based adjustment to your hydration goal. Your weight is not read from Apple Health — it's a profile value you enter directly in Sproutly (see above).

This data is governed by Apple's HealthKit privacy protections. Sproutly never transmits your Health data off your device, never sells it, never shares it with any third party, and never uses it for advertising or analytics of any kind — consistent with Apple's requirements for apps that use HealthKit. You can review or revoke Sproutly's Health access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Local notifications

Reminders (both "Smart Reminders" and "Custom Reminders") are scheduled entirely on your device using iOS's built-in local notification system. Sproutly does not use push notifications and has no notification server — nothing about your reminder schedule is ever sent anywhere.

Subscriptions and purchases

Sproutly Pro purchases are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see or store your payment details — no card number, no billing address. Apple shares only your subscription status (e.g., active or not) with the app, so it knows which features to unlock. See our Terms of Service for more on subscriptions.

Information we do NOT collect

  • No account creation, no email address, no name
  • No location data
  • No camera, photo library, or contacts access
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking software of any kind
  • No tracking of you across other apps or websites

2. How We Use Information

Because everything Sproutly stores lives on your own device, "use" is limited to running the app for you: calculating your hydration goal, showing your progress, syncing with Apple Health if enabled, and scheduling reminders. We do not use any information to build advertising profiles, to make automated decisions about you, or to sell to anyone — we simply don't have the infrastructure to do any of that, by design.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information — we don't hold a copy of it to begin with. The only limited circumstance in which anything could be disclosed is if we were required to do so by law, regulation, or valid legal process. If you contact us directly for support, we'll retain that correspondence (see Section 6) only to help resolve your request.

4. Third-Party Services

Sproutly does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. The only outside services involved are Apple's own:

5. Data Retention & Deletion

All hydration and profile data lives only on your device. Deleting the Sproutly app deletes this data along with it. Any data you've chosen to sync to Apple Health remains in the Health app under your control, governed by Apple's own retention and deletion tools (Health app → your profile → export or delete data), independent of Sproutly. If you email us for support, we keep that correspondence only as long as needed to help you, then delete it.

6. Data Security

Because your hydration and profile data never leaves your device, its security is largely handled by iOS itself — Apple's on-device encryption and sandboxing protect it the same way they protect any other app's local storage. We take reasonable care with any information you do send us directly (e.g., a support email), but no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

Since we don't collect or hold your personal data on any server, there is generally nothing on our end to access, correct, or delete beyond what you already control directly on your device or in the Health app. If you've contacted us by email, you can ask us to delete that correspondence at any time by writing to fernwayapps@gmail.com.

United Kingdom Residents (UK GDPR)

Fernway is based in the United Kingdom. Because Sproutly doesn't collect personal data on our own servers, our role as a "data controller" under the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 is minimal — limited to any correspondence you send us directly. For that correspondence, you have the rights to access, correct, or request erasure of it, to object to or restrict its processing, and to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator. Contact: Fernway, fernwayapps@gmail.com.

European Union / EEA Residents (GDPR)

The same limited role applies under the EU GDPR for residents of the European Economic Area — you have the rights to access, correct, or request erasure of any correspondence you've sent us, and to lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority. Contact: Fernway, fernwayapps@gmail.com.

California Residents (CCPA)

We do not sell or share personal information, and have none to disclose beyond what's described above. If this ever changes, we will update this policy and provide any required "Do Not Sell or Share" mechanism.

Indian Residents (DPDP)

For the same reason, our processing as a "data fiduciary" is limited to correspondence you send us. You may access, correct, or request erasure of that correspondence, or raise a grievance, by writing to fernwayapps@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Sproutly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because Sproutly has no accounts and no server-side data collection, this risk is inherently limited — but if you believe a child has contacted us directly with personal information, please write to fernwayapps@gmail.com and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add a new feature that changes what data Sproutly handles. We'll update the "Last Updated" date above, and for significant changes, we'll note it within the app. Continued use of Sproutly after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
